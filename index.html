01The Vision

The gap between people and their dreams isn't capability. It's daily consistency.

What DreamCoach is

DreamCoach is a dream operating system — not a habit tracker. While competitors track tasks and chores, DreamCoach operates at the level of meaning. It asks: what do you actually want your life to look like? Then it holds you in daily practice while that vision is built.

The product positions closer to premium digital coaching than habit tracking — unlocking a higher-value audience and a higher-value price point. Psychological depth (resistance archetypes, viability scoring, AI-guided coaching) wrapped in a dark, premium interface built for people who take themselves seriously.

Why now

The habit-tracking market was £1.7B in 2024 and is projected to reach £5.5B by 2033 at 14.2% CAGR. 58% of new apps now integrate AI — but most are doing it superficially.

The window for a psychologically grounded, AI-enhanced dream accountability app is open. The market is moving from toxic positivity toward genuine self-awareness tools. DreamCoach's psychological sophistication is ahead of where the category is going.

No app in this market addresses the full lifecycle of a dream — from articulation, through daily accountability, to either celebration or compassionate release. Market data · Straits Research / Global Growth Insights 2025

03The Solution

Built for the full lifecycle of a dream.

Four interconnected systems that work together. Miss one and the product doesn't land.

01 · Articulate

Name the dream.

Resistance archetype assessment, viability scoring, and a structured articulation process. The dream is defined with enough precision that daily action becomes possible.

Then
02 · Practise

Daily consistency.

One-question daily check-in. Streak system with built-in grace days. Points compound weekly and monthly. The dream becomes a habit — held, not chased.

Then

Held in community.

Dream Teams of up to five. Different dreams, shared accountability. Daily team feeds, supportive reactions, async advice. Privacy by design — no comparison pressure.

Then

Celebrate or release.

Reach the dream and celebrate it. Or recognise it no longer fits and walk away with dignity through the Compassionate Release framework. Both endings are wins.

05Dream Teams

Accountability through community. Privacy through design.

Group formation

Five people. Different dreams.

Users invite up to four others. Each person brings their own dream — career change, fitness shift, creative project, whatever matters. The team isn't built around a shared goal. It's built around shared discipline.

Daily shared updates

One line a day. Held by four others.

Each member posts a short update on their dream's progress. Members react and comment with support — not performance. Different dreams, no comparison, no competition for likes.

Async advice

Wisdom across dreams.

Team members can ask for advice on a moment of resistance, doubt, or decision. Replies arrive when teammates can give them — not in real time. A pace built for thinking, not for chat.

06Compassionate Release

The hardest dream to keep is the one that no longer fits.

Letting go isn't giving up. It's making room for what's next. DreamCoach is the only app in the category that treats the ending of a dream with the same care as the beginning.

i

Reflection — why this dream no longer serves you.

A structured prompt sequence. The user articulates what has changed — in their life, their values, their season — that makes this dream no longer the right one. No judgement. No nudge to stay.

ii

Recognition — what the dream taught you.

Every dream pursued leaves a mark. The user captures what they learned, what they gained, what shaped them. The dream is honoured for the work it did, even though it isn't where they're going.

iii

Permission — to walk away as a winner.

Letting go is reframed not as failure but as agency. The language of the app explicitly frames release as a sign of self-knowledge — not a defeat. This is the psychological pivot the rest of the framework rests on.

iv

Ritual — a small, deliberate act of release.

A guided ceremony inside the app. A written goodbye, a sealed envelope animation, a slow fade. The interaction is unhurried — this is the emotionally significant moment. Motion language is ceremonial here, not transactional.

v

Integration — what's next, or what's now.

The user can start a new dream, return to dreaming later, or sit in the clarity for a while. The released dream is archived (never deleted) — the user can revisit the work it did, anytime. Permission to return is given, explicitly.

People find it very difficult to let go because they tie the dream to their own identity and feel like a failure instead of a winner for letting go of something which doesn't suit them at this point in their life. David King-Reubens · Founder intake · May 2026

Glossary · What these numbers mean
LTV : CAC · 12.4×
Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost. For every £1 spent acquiring a user, the app earns £12.40 back over the user's lifetime. Anything above 3× is considered healthy. 12.4× means strong retention relative to acquisition cost.
Monthly Churn · 6%
The percentage of premium subscribers who cancel each month. Category average is 8–12%. At 6%, DreamCoach retains users better than most competitors. The grace day streak system and Dream Teams accountability are the structural reasons why.
Free → Premium · 4%
The percentage of free users who upgrade to a paid plan. Category average is 2–5%. The AI Dream Coach and the full Letting Go ritual are gated at Premium — they are the conversion drivers. The calculator above lets you model optimistic and conservative scenarios.
MAU
Monthly Active Users — the number of people who open the app at least once in a 30-day period. The industry standard denominator for all growth metrics. The starting point for every revenue calculation in the model.
Net ARPU
Average Revenue Per User, after payment processing. At £7.99/month list price, net ARPU is £7.19 after Stripe fees and the blended App Store / Play Store rate. What lands in the bank — not the headline price.
Avg Tenure · 14mo
How long the average premium subscriber stays before cancelling. At 14 months, users are paying for over a year. Dream Teams social accountability is the main driver — users in a team with friends are significantly less likely to cancel than solo users of any habit app.
MRR & ARR
Monthly and Annual Recurring Revenue. MRR is what premium subscribers pay each month. ARR is MRR × 12. These are the two numbers investors use to measure the business. At 50k MAU, 4% conversion, and £7.99 pricing: MRR ≈ £14k, ARR ≈ £173k. Scale to 500k MAU and the same rates return ≈ £1.7M MRR / £20M ARR.

Brand guidelines · DO and DON'T

+ Always do

  • Use uppercase + letter-spacing for all section labels
  • Emerald reserved for the semantic positive-state moment (Viable & Aligned, What Moved Today)
  • Whitespace as active design element — give content room
  • Gem/diamond as the iconographic foundation
  • Dark borders instead of shadows for depth
  • Off-white text (#E8EAF0), never pure white — reduces eye strain
  • WCAG AA verified on every colour combination

− Never do

  • Add gradients to backgrounds — keep surfaces flat
  • Use more than 2 accent colours in the same view
  • Round corners beyond 8px — it signals "friendly fintech"
  • Add decorative illustrations or mascots
  • Use Inter below 12px — switch to DM Mono for micro-labels
  • Break the uppercase label rule for sub-headings
  • Use emerald for anything that isn't semantic-positive
